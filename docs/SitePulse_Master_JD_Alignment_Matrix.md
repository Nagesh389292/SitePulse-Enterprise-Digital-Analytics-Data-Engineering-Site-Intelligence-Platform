## 3. The 30-Second Elevator Pitch (Role-Specific)

### For Senior Data Engineer (Content Ops & Site Quality)
> *"In digital analytics, data quality issues are usually silent—ETL pipelines report SUCCESS and schemas remain valid, but missing tracking tags quietly destroy marketing attribution and waste ad spend.
>
> In SitePulse, I engineered an end-to-end data pipeline using PySpark, Apache Iceberg, and Airflow. We ingest GA4-style clickstream streams, enforce schema contracts via a quarantine DLQ, and execute 30-minute inactivity sessionization and deduplication.
>
> We persist data into Apache Iceberg with ACID snapshot commits, enabling automated time-travel rollbacks. The pipeline feeds a columnar star schema where our dual-layer quality engine evaluates structural health and statistical anomalies, triggering AST-guarded AI Root Cause Analysis when reliability drops."*

### For Senior Data Analyst / Analytics Engineer
> *"When a frontend release accidentally breaks mobile checkout tracking, standard data pipelines don't fail, but mobile conversions collapse, causing immediate revenue leakage.
>
> I designed SitePulse to connect data engineering health with business decision-making. I engineered the functional requirements, mapped the As-Is vs To-Be process, and implemented a dual-layer reliability engine.
>
> While structural checks verify schemas and nulls, our Behavioral Anomaly Engine executes a pooled Two-Sample Proportion Z-Test. When mobile conversions diverge significantly from desktop (p < 0.05), SitePulse calculates the conversion gap, models the financial revenue exposure in dollars, opens an ITIL incident ticket, and provides Content Ops with a specific remediation plan within our target response window."*
